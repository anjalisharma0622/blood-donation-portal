About Blood Donation🩸

Why your donation matters

Blood cannot be manufactured in a lab — every unit used in a transfusion, surgery, or emergency comes from a voluntary donor. A single donation is separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets, meaning one visit can help up to three different patients.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Aged between 18 and 65 years
  • Weight Atleast 50 kg
  • Hemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL or higher
  • In good general health, free of active infection
  • Atleast 3 months since your last donation

Benefits of Donating Blood🩸

🩺

Free health check

Every donation includes a mini health screening — pulse, blood pressure, hemoglobin.


❤️

Heart health

Regular donation is linked to better iron balance and cardiovascular health.


🌍

Community impact

You directly help patients in your own city — accident victims, surgery patients, and more.


The Donation Process

  1. Registration: Fill a short form with your ID and basic details.
  2. Screening: A quick check of hemoglobin, blood pressure, and pulse.
  3. Donation: The actual draw takes about 8–10 minutes.
  4. Rest & Refreshments: Relax for 10–15 minutes before you leave.
