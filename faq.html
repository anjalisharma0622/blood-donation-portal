Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate blood? +

Most healthy adults aged 18–65, weighing at least 50 kg, with hemoglobin above 12.5 g/dL, can donate. A quick screening at the donation site confirms eligibility on the day.

How often can I donate? +

Whole blood donation is generally safe every 3 months, giving your body enough time to replenish red blood cells and iron stores.

Is blood donation safe? +

Yes. All equipment is sterile and single-use, handled by trained medical staff, so there's no risk of infection to the donor.

How long does it take? +

The full visit — registration, screening, donation, and rest — takes about 30–45 minutes. The actual blood draw is only 8–10 minutes.

Will donating make me weak? +

Most donors feel completely normal within a few hours. Eating well and staying hydrated before and after donating helps you recover faster.
